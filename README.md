# 📊 Business Performance Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Business Performance Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Tableau dashboard developed to analyze retail business performance. It provides a comprehensive overview of key business metrics, including Sales, Profit, Discount, Shipping Cost, and Order Count. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor performance, identify profitable product categories, analyze sales trends, and make data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall business performance using key KPIs.
- Analyze sales and profit across different product categories.
- Identify the highest and lowest profitable sub-categories.
- Track monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Understand the impact of discounts on profitability.
- Support business decision-making through interactive visualizations.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Discount
- Total Shipping Cost
- Total Orders

### Profit by Sub-Category
Displays the profit generated by each product sub-category to identify the best and worst performing products.

### Sales by Category
Shows the contribution of Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology to total sales.

### Sales vs Profit by Year
Compares yearly sales and profit to evaluate business growth.

### Monthly Sales Trend
Visualizes monthly sales performance to identify seasonal trends.

### Profit vs Discount
Analyzes the relationship between discount and profit using a scatter plot.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Technology generated the highest sales.
- Copiers were the most profitable sub-category.
- Sales and profit consistently increased from 2016 to 2019.
- Higher discounts generally reduced profitability.
- Sales showed seasonal trends, with higher performance in the final months of the year.
